Legal notice / Privacy

This text describes this static page. It is not legal advice.

Scope

The page is a private overview, in French and English, for its publisher in Quebec. It is not offered to the public in the European Union. GDPR and the ePrivacy directive are therefore not treated here as the applicable law. If people in the European Union are later invited, the analysis has to be done again.

The Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (CQLR c. P-39.1, amended by Bill 25) is the reference text. The federal PIPEDA applies to commercial activity. Quebec has a statute treated as substantially similar for collection that stays inside the province. The Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada states that commercial processing which crosses a border remains subject to PIPEDA. Hosting the traffic at Cloudflare sends information outside Quebec. The publisher decides how to classify the activity.

Cookies

The page sets no cookie. It loads no font and no script from another site. There is no banner: nothing to accept beyond signing in.

After a successful Access login, Cloudflare sets CF_Authorization. That cookie carries the session, here 24 hours, so the code is not asked again on every request. It is required for that authentication. It is not a measurement cookie.

Section 9.1 requires the highest privacy level by default for a technological service offered to the public; its second paragraph sets cookie privacy settings aside. Section 8.1 covers technologies that identify, locate or profile: they do not turn on by themselves. The Commission d’accès à l’information describes the same default for cookies that profile, identify or locate.

Hosting

The chosen host is Cloudflare Workers, name oxinote-prive, on the account’s workers.dev subdomain, with no custom domain. Version URLs are off (preview_urls false). Cloudflare runs a network outside Quebec.

Before communicating personal information outside Quebec, section 17 requires a privacy impact assessment and a written agreement. The same requirement applies when someone outside Quebec is entrusted with collection, use or storage. This page is not that assessment.

Rights

The Private Sector Act provides, among other things, for a copy of information held, including in a structured technological format where section 27 so provides, for rectification (section 28), for withdrawal of consent to use or communication (section 8), and, in the cases of section 28.1, for cessation of dissemination or de-indexing.

A request for access or rectification is in writing and addressed to the person responsible (section 30). The answer is in writing, within 30 days (section 32). A refusal is explained (section 34).

The published channel is the profile github.com/LeVibeux. Say that the request is about personal information on the OxiNote page, and give a way to confirm identity without sending a full identity document if a narrower means is enough.

A complaint can also go to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec. For PIPEDA: the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Measures and incidents

The page is meant to sit behind Cloudflare Access (one email address, a one-time code, a 24-hour session), with no advertised domain, and with a tag asking crawlers not to index it. Section 10 requires reasonable measures given sensitivity, purpose, quantity, distribution and medium.

An incident with a risk of serious injury is reported to the Commission and to the persons concerned (sections 3.5 and following). The person to reach is LeVibeux, via github.com/LeVibeux.
